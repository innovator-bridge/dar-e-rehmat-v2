Who we are

Dar-e-Rehmat is a family promise, named for the sake of our grandfather Muhammad Hayat. No one in our town should go hungry. Every woman should be able to learn a skill and stand on her own. And no patient should be left without a way to reach care.

The need

What our neighbours face

01

Hunger is daily, not occasional

Children, women and elders in our town often go without a proper midday meal.

02

Women work hard with no ladder up

Many mothers work in other people’s homes. Their daughters have no route to a skill of their own.

03

The hospital is far away

For a sick villager, getting to a good hospital can be harder than the illness itself.

04

Good care is out of reach

Qualified doctors, scans and medicines are rarely within a family’s means.

05

Aid alone does not last

Handing out help is not enough. People need a way to stand on their own feet.

Our programmes

Three programmes, one purpose

01

Langar Khana

A hot meal for anyone who comes, served at midday in a clean hall, with dignity.

  • 280 to 300 people served daily
  • 60 to 70 women every day
Read more →
02

Stitching Centre

Each afternoon, after the meal, women and girls learn to sew garments and become self-sufficient.

  • About 30 girls training now
  • Self-sufficient in six months
Read more →
03

Transport and Medical Aid

Every morning our car carries patients from Rajajang to a hospital nearby and brings them home.

  • One hospital trip each morning
  • Consultations with qualified doctors

How the work has grown

20 December 2025

The kitchen opens

The first pots go on and the midday meal begins. It has not stopped since.

Soon after

Women and children join the table

Daily numbers climb to 280 to 300, with 60 to 70 women, many with their children.

Every morning

The hospital trip

A seven-seater car takes patients to hospital for doctors, scans and medicines, then brings them home. Nearly 600 people have been supported.

Every afternoon

The stitching centre

Once the diners leave, the same hall becomes a classroom for about 30 girls.
