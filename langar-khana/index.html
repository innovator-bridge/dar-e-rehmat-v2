Langar Khana

A hot midday meal for 280 to 300 people every day, served with dignity and never closed.

The kitchen opens every day at midday. Children, women and elders sit together on clean mats and eat. Around 40 of the 60 to 70 women who come each day bring their children to dine, and the rest take packed food home for the little ones.
