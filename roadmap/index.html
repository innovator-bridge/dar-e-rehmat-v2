From learner to teacher

How a woman with no skill becomes self-sufficient in six months, and then trains others.

The roadmap

Five steps to independence

  1. 01
    Day one

    Join the centre

    After the midday meal, women and their daughters start training in our hall.

  2. 02
    Months 1 to 6

    Train, 3 hours a day, 6 days a week

    About 470 hours of practice, first on cloth and thread, then on machines.

  3. 03
    Month 7

    Fully trained

    She can stitch professionally and no longer needs training.

  4. 04
    Within a month

    Earn and own her machine

    Working 3 to 4 hours a day she earns at least $5 and buys her own machine.

  5. 05
    Then

    Train others

    Some of our graduates in town already train other women, and the circle grows.
