The Stitching Centre

Every afternoon, after the midday meal, the same hall becomes a classroom where women and girls learn a skill that lasts a lifetime.

The stitching model

From first stitch to own machine

Our food programme feeds people today. Our stitching centre is different. It is built so that each woman leaves needing us less.

01

Come after the meal

When the midday meal ends, the hall turns into a classroom. Maids bring their daughters here to train.

Day one
02

Learn on cloth and thread

Beginners practise by hand on ordinary cloth, with thread and needle, guided by our volunteer teacher.

Months 1 to 6
03

Sew real garments and earn

Trained women move to electric machines and produce garments. Larger work can come from companies that give clothing to be stitched.

After training
04

Own machine, own income

With a skill and an income, a woman can buy her own machine, work from home and move on. A whole family stands a little taller.

Independence

Open to every girl

Maids and their daughters are welcome to train here.

Taught with care

A volunteer teacher guides every girl.

Built to last

A skill is something no one can take away.
