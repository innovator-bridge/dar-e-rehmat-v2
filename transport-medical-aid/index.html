Transport and medical aid

A ride to the hospital, every morning

Each morning our seven-seater car takes patients from Rajajang to Indus Hospital in Jubilee Town, Lahore, with a driver we hire for the work. They see highly qualified doctors, get full body scans and receive free medicines, including specialist ones, and then we bring them home.

  • One trip every morning
  • Seven-seater car
  • Nearly 600 people supported
  • Doctors, scans and free medicines
The daily route

Dar-e-Rehmat, Rajajang → Indus Hospital, Jubilee Town

Open in Google Maps

About 27 km by road, roughly half an hour each way. The route and the hospital pin are approximate.
